FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the assistant understand Persian?

Yes. Write in Persian or English and it replies in the same language. It can create and update tasks, habits, goals, reminders, notes and brainstorm items for you.

Will I get notifications when the app is closed?

On Android and desktop Chromium browsers, yes, enable Web Push in Settings → Notifications. You can mark a task done or snooze a reminder right from the notification. In-app alerts always work.

How are goals measured?

Pick a measure: a number (works for decreasing targets too), milestones from sub-goals, completed linked tasks, or a manual 0–100. Neura shows your pace against the time elapsed.

Can I connect notes to my tasks and goals?

Every note can live in a folder and link to a goal, a task and a list. Open a goal and you'll see its notes, tasks and reminders together.

Which day does my week start on?

Your choice, Saturday, Sunday or Monday. The calendar, habit grids and dashboard all follow it.
